# BRI ALDER
*Wick's sister. Introduced B01 c01 (off-page). On-page B02 c008.*

## FACTS
- 19 at B02. Left Hollow Alder Rust Y-810 with a bag, an unfiled licence-application, and 9s.
- Kindled at fourteen: **thirteen feet, brick.** Camber said *well now* out loud in front of
  the village. Coherence **settled**; weight ~2.5.
- Loam still under the Marrowfield in her voice. Wick has "Dad's neck"; she noticed in a second.
- Lost a Hall runner's job in Green Y-811 for taking a shilling out of a purse she was carrying.
  She was hungry. She says it without pause and makes nothing of it, and has not done it again.
- Swept Doreth Faring's workshop floor for seven months at 3s a week and never once asked for
  more. Now paid 30s a month and keep.

## WHAT SHE IS
**The second-best fitter in Marrowfield, and the best inside two years**, and she knows it the
way you know a number rather than the way people know things about themselves. **A hundred and
forty fittings, every one entered under Doreth's name.** On no register and never will be: the
Concord admits at fourteen, indentures for seven years, and requires an £11 bond, and she was
seventeen with nine shillings and no family in the town. *There is no door that opens at eighteen.*

## VOICE
Direct to the point of bluntness, and completely without self-pity — she decided years ago that
shame was not worth the storage. Answers the hard question first. Undercuts her own sentences.
Uses "that's not an excuse and it's not not one" constructions: two true things, in either order.

## THE THING THAT MAKES HER USEFUL TO THE SERIES
**She reads people the way Wick reads ground.** A fitter's whole job is *where*, not what — she
can feel where a graft wants to sit and cannot say how. She asked Wick **"where does it sit?"**
in B02 c008 and could not explain the question. **Do not resolve this before Book 4.** It is the
outside view of his own problem and he will need it.

## HARD LIMITS
- She is **not in trouble** and must never retroactively become a rescue plot.
- She is **not coming home to live**, and that stands.
- Her gift must never be explained as secret rank or hidden talent. It is two years of arms.
