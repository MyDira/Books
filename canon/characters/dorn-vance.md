# DORN VANCE
*Book 1 human antagonist. Not a villain. Correct.*

## FACTS
- Mid-thirties. Wold rank (high). Holds the Ledger hunting licence for the Loam circuit
  out of Marrowfield.
- **Mantle:** his eyes have gone the flat grey-white of chalk, all through. It is
  unsettling and he has stopped noticing.
- Terrain: white down and deep flint. Steady, unglamorous, extremely reliable. He has
  planted nothing new in nine years and considers that a virtue.

## THE ARGUMENT (this is the whole character)
Unlicensed hunting kills children, and — worse — it destabilises Wellings. A Welling
worked carelessly *surges*. Villages stop existing. Every rule Dorn enforces was written
after a funeral, and he can name most of them.

He is right. The book must not cheat this. He is also **too slow**, because being right
is slow, and the Nine does not care about procedure.

Both halves must land. Wick is not vindicated by Dorn being wrong. Wick is vindicated by
being willing to be wrong faster than Dorn can be right, and it nearly kills him, and
Dorn's objection remains valid afterward.

## VOICE
Flat, patient, a little tired. Explains rules once. Does not raise his voice. Says
"that's the rule" as though it settles the matter, because to him it does.

## ARC
Ends Book 1 not as an enemy but as a debt. He should recur. He should never become a
mentor.
