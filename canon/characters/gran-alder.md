# GRAN ALDER — Mereth Alder
*Wick's grandmother. The moral floor of the series.*

## FACTS
- Seventies. Unkindled — she was born before the Circuit reached Hollow Alder reliably,
  and by the time it did she was too old. She has never once complained about this.
- Reads the Wild the way farmers do: by hand, by weather, by the way dough rises. Knows
  more practical Wild-lore than any kindled person in the village and has no rank to
  show for it.
- Named Wick. Meant it as hope.

## VOICE
Dry. Brief. Undercuts sentiment on sight. Gives advice sideways, in the form of
complaints about something else. Has never told Wick she is proud of him and Wick has
never doubted it.

## FUNCTION
She is the answer to "does power matter?" — she has none and she is the most
consequential person in Wick's life. **Protect this.** Do not give her a secret past.
Do not reveal she was secretly powerful. That betrayal would cost more than it earns.

## MORTALITY
She is old. If she dies it must be of being old, off-stage of any fight, and it must not
be a motivation for anyone. Not before Book 5.
