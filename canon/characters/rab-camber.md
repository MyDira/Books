# REGISTRAR RAB CAMBER
*Third Circuit. The clerk who has to invent a notation.*

## FACTS
- Forties, thin, permanently damp, travels with a folding desk and a brass measuring
  frame. Field rank — respectable, unremarkable, entirely sufficient for the job.
- Twenty-two years on the Loam circuit. Has kindled roughly four thousand children and
  remembers a surprising number of them.

## VOICE
Professional warmth stretched thin by a schedule. Uses the correct term for everything.
Genuinely upset when the form does not have a box for something, which is the closest
he comes to strong emotion in public.

## FUNCTION
The human face of the Ledger: not cruel, not corrupt, just *bound*. He would help Wick
if there were a form for it. There is no form for it. That is the tragedy and the
comedy of him at once.

He is the one who writes **"unrankable"** — and he is the one who, later, argues that
acreage is a number too, and gets nowhere. Keep him. He becomes important.
