# NETTLE — Annet Faring
*Co-lead. The inverse problem.*

## FACTS
- Age 15 at introduction (B01 c13). Marrowfield-born; mother a Grafter, father unknown
  and not discussed.
- Small, square, dark, permanently scowling; a burn scar across the left forearm from her
  mother's workshop. Hair cropped short with a knife, badly, by herself.
- Called Nettle since she was six. She prefers it. Anyone who uses "Annet" is either
  family or about to regret it.
- Kindled at fourteen. Her Hold opened **two feet across** — a slab of ground struck by
  lightning so often the sand has turned to glass — and **heavier than anything the
  Marrowfield office had ever recorded at Ember.**

## THE PROBLEM
Weight without room. She hits harder than practitioners three ranks above her and she
cannot advance, because there is nowhere to put anything. One terrain, no borders, no
edges. Every Seed she has tried to plant has been rejected or cracked her. She is
**capped**, at fifteen, and everybody in Marrowfield has told her so kindly.

She is not sad about it. She is furious about it, which is more useful and much worse
company.

## VOICE
Short. Rude. Precise. Answers in three words when three words will do and in one word
when they won't. Never explains herself. Occasionally says something devastatingly
perceptive and then denies having said it. Does not do reassurance in either direction.

## WITH WICK
They have exactly opposite deficits and neither of them can see it for a long time. She
sees a boy with more ground than he deserves and no idea what to do with it; he sees
someone who can actually *do things*. The friendship is built on work before it is built
on liking, which is why it holds.

**Do not make this a romance before Book 9.** It can be one eventually. It must be a
partnership first, and the partnership must never stop being the point.
