# CONTINUITY LOG
*Append-only, chapter by chapter. Every concrete fact established in text: names, dates,
distances, injuries, promises made by characters, objects acquired, weather, who was
present. This is the archaeological record that lets a future session verify a claim
without rereading a million words.*

*Format: one block per chapter. Terse. Facts only, no summary of plot beats — that's
what `canon/STATE.md` and the book OUTLINE are for.*
