# Book 01 — WEIGHTLESS
*Draft outline v1 — pending story-architect pass.*

**Logline:** A boy whose Hold opens forty-one acres wide and completely empty has six
months to be worth measuring, or the Ledger strikes him off for life.

**New system idea:** Acreage is the number nobody can buy — and an empty Hold can take
a Seed that would rupture anyone else, because there is nothing in it to crack.

**Want (concrete, checkable):** Be assessed at **Hearth** by the Rust circuit. The
Circuit visits the Loam twice a year. Miss it unrankable a second time and Wick is
recorded **Failed of Kindling** — permanent, appealable in theory, never overturned in
practice. No Hunt licence, no Hall, no craft apprenticeship, ever. Clock: Green → Rust.
Six months.

**Antagonist (the argument):** **Dorn Vance**, Wold-rank licensed hunter out of
Marrowfield, holds the Loam hunting licence. He is *right*: unlicensed hunting kills
children and destabilises Wellings into surges that eat villages. Every rule he enforces
exists because someone died. He is also too slow, because being right is slow.

**Antagonist (the monster):** the **Nine-in-a-Coat** — nine tallow-crawlers grown
together into one thing that wants a shape, and wears what it has eaten. Wold grade.
Loam folklore says it knocks. A thing made of many small things that became one thing:
the exact inverse of Wick's problem, which is that he is one large thing made of nothing.

**Setting:** Hollow Alder and the unlicensed Welling under the ford. Marrowfield in the
back third. Thin ground throughout — this is a book about scarcity.

## ACT STRUCTURE (32 chapters)
- **Act I — ch 1–8 · THE MEASUREMENT.** Hollow Alder, thin ground, the Wild as domestic
  weather. The kindling. Forty-one acres of nothing. The clerk inventing a notation.
  The village's kindness, which is worse than cruelty. Wick decides.
- **Turn 1 — ch 8:** He finds the seam under the ford. An unlicensed Welling. He does not
  report it, and he knows exactly what that makes him.
- **Act II-a — ch 9–16 · THE THIN EDGE.** Hunting tallow-crawlers alone with a spear.
  The first Seed. Planting it. It is worth four copper and no respect, and it is the
  best thing that has ever happened to him. Nettle arrives, hunting the same seam for
  the opposite reason. Pell arrives to audit a Welling that is not on any map.
- **Midpoint — ch 16 · THE REVERSAL:** The thing Wick believes — that he needs to fill
  his Hold fast — becomes false. He learns what a rupture is by watching one, and learns
  that his emptiness is the only reason he is still alive. The disadvantage inverts.
- **Act II-b — ch 17–24 · WHAT IS IT FOR.** The white down. Real hunting. Dorn Vance
  shuts them down and is right to. The Welling is growing on a schedule. Something in it
  is *organising*.
- **Turn 2 — ch 24 · LOW POINT:** The knocking starts in the village. Someone is taken.
  Dorn goes in by the book and it is not enough. Wick is struck off early — or the
  Circuit is called away, and the clock runs out with him still at zero.
- **Act III — ch 25–32 · THE COAT.** Into the Welling. The Nine. A Wold-grade fight a
  Hearth cannot win by strength, only by geography. The Seed that should rupture him
  and does not, because there is nothing in him to crack — it lands forty acres out in
  the dark and starts walking toward the middle.
- **Landing — ch 32:** Assessment. Hearth. A tally with real numbers on it. And a
  terrain nobody in the Register has seen, still arriving.
