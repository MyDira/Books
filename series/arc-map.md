## THE FIVE CURRENCIES
1. **Rank** — primary B1–5, then increasingly scarce.
2. **Settled acreage and terrain build** — runs all 25 books. Wick's signature. The
   reason his progression stays interesting when the ladder slows.
3. **Coherence** — only three grades in the entire series (plus Ledger sub-classes,
   which are free and funny). Deliberately scarce, so each is enormous.
4. **Notion → Law → Writ** — primary B6–20. A practitioner may hold more than one Law,
   which makes this an *unbounded* currency.
5. **Scope of consequence** — primary B16–25. The same power aimed at a larger thing.

## KEEPING LATE BOOKS FAST
1. **Shorten the story-clock as ranks lengthen.** B1 spans six months; B20 spans nine days.
2. **The grind may pass between books; the deed never may.** Drawing and walking can
   happen in gaps. Every *step* is earned on the page.
3. **Split the step.** From March up, one rank = three visible internal milestones.
4. **Advance the crew.** From ~B8, Nettle taking Wold is an event the reader counts.
   Six people on their own ladders multiplies ratchets at zero cost to Wick's runway.
5. **Raise number resolution, not magnitude.** Early books count acres in ones; late
   books in hundreds, and the Ledger invents new units.
6. **Punch up one gap per movement.** Gaps are qualitative; a Realm beating a Dominion
   thrills exactly because the reader knows the arithmetic.

## THE SEAM (long burn opened B01 c31)
The Nine's Seed. Its dominant terrain is *the thing that makes many into one*: an edge.
In a room-sized Hold it would rupture. In forty-one acres of open salt it arrives slowly.
- **It is a timer, not a mystery.** Wick can measure the distance. It shrinks by a
  stateable amount. Camber files it. There is a form.
- **It costs him.** Every acre it crosses is ground he cannot plant. His single advantage
  inverts on the last page of Book 1.
- **Waypoints:** B3 climax it crosses a boundary and makes a terrain stop working.
- **Lands end of B5, at the Assize**, in public, while he is being formally measured.
- **What it is:** not a creature, not a friend — a **horizon**. The thing Field rank
  requires, arriving uninvited, dividing ground he needed whole. A fence line drawn by
  someone else.
- It points at `bible/08`: the Sill is a managed depth. Wick acquires, at fourteen, a
  working model of the thing the series is finally about.
- **Hard rule 1 holds.** It never becomes a friend, a mentor, or a power-up.
